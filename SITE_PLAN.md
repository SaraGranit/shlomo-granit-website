# Rabbi Shlomo Granit - Website Plan

Status: Draft for approval. Planning only; implementation has not started.
Date: 2026-10-08
Proposed scope: Five primary pages.

## 1. Language and Scope

- All project documentation, filenames, code identifiers, and comments will be in English.
- The proposed visitor-facing website language is Hebrew, with right-to-left (RTL) layout. This is separate from the project-file language requirement and should be confirmed before implementation.
- Before development, agree on how approved Hebrew content will be supplied and managed while keeping project files in English, such as through a content management system.
- The English spelling "Shlomo Granit" is provisional and requires approval.
- This document defines the initial website structure, content requirements, visual direction, and delivery stages. It does not authorize deployment or select a technology stack.

## 2. Purpose

Create a professional, approachable website that introduces the rabbi, explains his activities, and helps visitors make an appropriate inquiry.

The four confirmed activity areas are:

1. Torah classes and lectures.
2. Conducting wedding ceremonies (chuppah).
3. Counseling and mediation.
4. Marital harmony and education.

The website should communicate warmth, credibility, and clarity without exaggerated claims. Biography, qualifications, testimonials, service locations, and availability must be approved before publication.

Working assumption: Visitors primarily need to understand the services and contact the rabbi, rather than book or pay online. Confirm this before development.

## 3. Audiences

| Proposed audience | Main need | Intended journey |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Communities, institutions, and event organizers | Arrange a class or lecture | Review topics, understand the format, make an inquiry |
| Couples preparing for marriage | Find a rabbi to conduct their wedding | Get acquainted, understand the process, ask about a date |
| People seeking guidance or help resolving a disagreement | Understand counseling and mediation options | Review the scope, request an introductory conversation |
| Couples and parents | Seek guidance on marriage, family, or education | Identify the relevant area, make a discreet initial inquiry |

Audience descriptions are initial assumptions for approval, not claims about existing clients.

## 4. Sitemap

| Page | Proposed route | Purpose | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | Introduce the rabbi and guide visitors to an activity | Explore services or get in touch |
| Classes and Lectures | `/lectures` | Present topics and speaking opportunities | Request a class or lecture |
| Wedding Ceremonies | `/weddings` | Introduce the approach and coordination process | Ask about a wedding date |
| Counseling and Mediation | `/counseling-mediation` | Explain the scope and process | Request an introductory conversation |
| Marital Harmony and Education | `/family-education` | Present marriage, parenting, and educational guidance | Make a family-related inquiry |

Five pages give each activity a clear destination without overcrowding the home page. A separate About page is unnecessary initially: the biography belongs on Home. Contact details and a contact section will be available on every page instead of adding a sixth primary page.

Privacy and accessibility information may require auxiliary documents linked from the footer. These are outside the five primary content pages; their scope depends on the final features and applicable requirements.

## 5. Page Specifications

### 5.1 Home

Goal: Quickly establish who the rabbi is, what he does, and how to contact him.

Recommended content order:

1. Opening section with the rabbi's name, an approved introduction, and an authentic portrait.
2. A primary contact action and a secondary link to the activity overview.
3. Four concise service summaries linking to the dedicated pages.
4. A short biography covering approved background and personal approach.
5. An optional featured lecture, activity photo, or approved testimonial.
6. Contact section and footer.

Required materials: Portrait, short biography, approved contact details, and any suitable supporting media. Do not fabricate missing content.

### 5.2 Classes and Lectures

Goal: Help organizers understand what they can arrange and whether it suits their audience.

Recommended content:

- Introduction and an authentic photo from a class or lecture, with publication permission.
- Topics supplied by the rabbi, grouped only when the actual list warrants it.
- Intended audiences, available formats, and typical duration, subject to confirmation.
- Selected video or audio examples, if available and cleared for publication.
- Practical questions about scheduling, location, and equipment.
- A clear action to request a class or lecture.

Useful inquiry details: Name, contact method, desired topic, approximate date, and location. Only name and one contact method should be required if a form is used.

Do not assume remote sessions, nationwide travel, additional languages, or particular lecture topics.

### 5.3 Wedding Ceremonies

Goal: Help couples get acquainted with the rabbi and understand the coordination process.

Recommended content:

- Personal introduction and an authentic wedding photo, subject to permission from the couple and photographer.
- The rabbi's approach to conducting a chuppah, in wording he approves.
- The actual sequence of inquiry, introductory meeting, preparation, and ceremony, once confirmed.
- Practical information about locations, coordination, and what the service includes.
- Frequently asked questions and optional approved testimonials.
- A clear action to ask about a wedding date.

Useful inquiry details: Name, contact method, date, and venue if known. An inquiry does not reserve a date or confirm a booking.

Verify any relevant authorization, credentials, and official coordination requirements before publishing claims about wedding services.

### 5.4 Counseling and Mediation

Goal: Explain the guidance and dispute-resolution services actually offered.

Recommended content:

- Confirmed areas of counseling and mediation.
- A clear distinction between individual guidance and mediation, if both are offered.
- How an introductory conversation and subsequent meetings work.
- Verified relevant background and qualifications.
- Practical questions about format and coordination.
- A clear action to request an introductory conversation.

Content boundary: Focus on the counseling and mediation areas confirmed by the rabbi. Discuss marriage, parenting, and education primarily on the family page, with cross-links where appropriate.

Privacy boundary: Do not request dispute narratives, information about other parties, or personal documents through the initial contact form. Do not promise absolute confidentiality without an established basis.

### 5.5 Marital Harmony and Education

Goal: Present family guidance in a sensitive, respectful, and nonjudgmental way.

Recommended content:

- An approved introduction to the rabbi's approach.
- Two clearly distinguished sections: marital harmony, and parenting and education.
- Specific subjects on which guidance is offered, supplied by the rabbi.
- What the initial conversation and follow-up process involve.
- Frequently asked questions about suitability and meeting format.
- A clear action to make a family-related inquiry.

Content boundary: Do not imply clinical treatment, professional qualifications, or guaranteed outcomes that have not been verified. The site is not an emergency response channel; suitable wording should be considered once the service scope is confirmed.

Privacy boundary: Do not collect children's details or sensitive family histories in the initial form. Publish no identifiable case stories without explicit approval.

## 6. Navigation and Contact

- Header: Rabbi's name, links to the five pages, and a clear contact action.
- Mobile navigation: Accessible menu with stable controls and keyboard support.
- Footer: Approved contact details, navigation, and relevant auxiliary documents.
- Contact actions should lead to the current page's contact section or an approved direct contact channel.
- Proposed direct channels: Telephone and WhatsApp, subject to confirmation of the numbers and preferred channels.
- WhatsApp links may include a generic service-specific message, but no sensitive or personal information in the URL.
- A contact form is optional, not a prerequisite for launch. If approved, request name, phone or email, and service category. Date and location may be optional for lectures and weddings.
- Forms need accessible validation, submitting, success, and failure states. Show success only after the receiving service confirms acceptance; provide an alternative contact method on failure.
- Confirm the recipient, access permissions, retention period, privacy wording, and accessible spam protection before enabling a form.
- Do not promise response times or publish office hours unless approved.

## 7. Visual Direction

Character: Personal, dignified, contemporary, and calm.

- Proposed palette: Off-white background, charcoal text, deep green accents, and restrained gold details, with accessible contrast.
- Typography: Consider Frank Ruhl Libre for Hebrew headings and Heebo for body text, subject to existing branding and font licensing.
- Home opening: A real, prominent photo with the rabbi's name and a brief introduction placed in a readable area. Use a mobile crop or layout that preserves both the face and text.
- Keep a hint of the next section visible in the first viewport; avoid an oversized opening that hides the services.
- Use open content sections and clear spacing. Reserve cards for repeated items such as service summaries, not entire sections or nested containers.
- Prefer authentic activity photographs over generic stock imagery. Arrange photography if suitable assets are unavailable.
- Keep motion restrained and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Avoid invented testimonials, unverified credentials, decorative religious claims, and overly promotional language.

## 8. Functional and Quality Requirements

### Responsive Layout and Accessibility

- Support mobile, tablet, and desktop without horizontal overflow or overlapping content.
- Use Hebrew language metadata and RTL layout for the proposed public site, with correct handling of phone numbers and email addresses.
- Provide semantic headings, keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible form labels, and appropriate image descriptions.
- Support text enlargement and comfortable touch targets.
- Review video and audio accessibility, including captions or text alternatives as appropriate.
- Target WCAG 2.2 AA during design and development. Review applicable obligations before launch; do not claim verified compliance in advance.

### Performance, Search, and Privacy

- Optimize images for screen size and defer below-the-fold media. Avoid autoplay.
- Provide a unique title and description, one main heading, stable URLs, and appropriate sharing metadata for each page.
- Supply a search-engine sitemap. Mention service locations only after confirmation.
- Use HTTPS and keep service secrets out of browser-delivered code.
- Do not send personal details or inquiry contents to analytics. Analytics and third-party media embeds require an explicit decision and privacy review.
- Test actual inquiry delivery, access restrictions, and retention settings before enabling collection.

### Maintenance

- Select the technology after confirming who maintains the content and how frequently it changes.
- A mostly static site may need only a lightweight implementation; frequent lecture publishing may justify a content management system.
- Confirm domain ownership, hosting ownership, maintenance responsibilities, and backup arrangements.

## 9. Out of Scope for the First Release

- Online payments.
- Live appointment or wedding booking calendars.
- User accounts or case management.
- A blog, comprehensive lecture library, or advanced search.
- A multilingual public website.
- Automated personal counseling through a chatbot.

Selected recordings may still appear on the lectures page without creating a full media library.

1. Approve this plan, page structure, service boundaries, and contact channels.
2. Gather materials and write content for the rabbi's approval.
3. Prepare desktop and mobile designs for Home and one service page.
4. Agree on the technology, hosting, and content maintenance approach.

- All four activities have dedicated pages linked from Home and the navigation.
- Each page has a visible, relevant contact action with verified destinations.
- All five pages work on mobile and desktop without clipped text, overflow, or overlapping controls.
- Navigation and contact controls work with a keyboard; any form is tested for validation, successful delivery, and failure handling.
- All biographical claims, images, and testimonials are approved, with no placeholder details remaining.
- Each page has appropriate search and sharing metadata.
- Required auxiliary documents and maintenance arrangements are ready before launch.
- Project files follow the English-language requirement; the public-content language and its storage approach have been agreed.
